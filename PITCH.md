# NegoRide - Investor Pitch

## 🎯 Executive Summary

NegoRide is Liberia's first fare-negotiation ride-hailing and delivery platform. Unlike traditional ride-hailing apps with fixed pricing and surge fees, NegoRide empowers passengers to set their own price and negotiate directly with drivers. This unique model creates fair pricing for passengers and better earnings for drivers.

## 💡 The Problem

### For Passengers
- **Surge Pricing** - Apps like Uber/Bolt charge 2-3x during peak hours
- **No Control** - Cannot negotiate or choose based on budget
- **Hidden Fees** - Final price often higher than quoted
- **Limited Options** - Fixed prices don't consider individual budgets

### For Drivers
- **Low Earnings** - Platform commissions eat into profits
- **Surge Manipulation** - Algorithms control when they can earn more
- **No Flexibility** - Must accept platform-set prices
- **Penalty for Declining** - Ratings affected for refusing rides

### In Liberia Specifically
- **Cash-Based Economy** - Many apps don't support local mobile money well
- **Limited Transport Options** - Not all apps support motorcycles (bodas) or kehkeh
- **Foreign Ownership** - Existing apps are foreign-owned with less local understanding
- **High Unemployment** - Need for flexible income opportunities

## ✅ Our Solution

**NegoRide: Your Ride, Your Price**

### Core Innovation: Fare Negotiation
Passengers set their offer price → Drivers accept or counter-offer → Both agree on fair price

### Key Benefits
- **No Surge Pricing** - Passengers always control the price
- **Fair Earnings** - Drivers negotiate rates that work for them
- **Transparent** - Price agreed before ride starts
- **Flexible** - Decline requests without penalty

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Driver Adoption
- **Mitigation**: Lower commission (10%), sign-up bonuses, flexible schedule

### Risk 2: User Education (Negotiation Concept)
- **Mitigation**: In-app tutorials, suggested prices based on distance, clear UI

### Risk 3: Competition Response
- **Mitigation**: First-mover advantage in negotiation, rapid expansion, brand loyalty

### Risk 4: Payment Integration Challenges
- **Mitigation**: Partner with MTN/Orange, cash option as backup

### Risk 5: Safety Concerns
- **Mitigation**: Strict driver verification, SOS button, live tracking, insurance
