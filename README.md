# NegoRide - Liberia's Fair Ride-Hailing App

## 🚗 Your Ride, Your Price

NegoRide is Liberia's innovative ride-hailing and delivery app that puts power back in your hands. Unlike traditional ride-hailing services with fixed pricing and surge fees, NegoRide allows passengers to set their own fare and negotiate directly with drivers.

## ✨ Key Features

### For Passengers
- **Set Your Own Price** - Offer the fare you're willing to pay
- **Fare Negotiation** - Drivers can accept or make counter-offers
- **Choose Your Driver** - Compare ratings, vehicle types, and arrival times
- **Multiple Transport Options** - Cars, motorcycles (bodas), and kehkeh (tuk-tuks)
- **Real-Time Tracking** - Live GPS tracking on every trip
- **Flexible Payments** - MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, Cash (LRD/USD)

### For Drivers
- **Fair Earnings** - Accept or negotiate fares that work for you
- **Flexible Schedule** - Go online when you want to earn
- **No Surge Pricing Tricks** - Transparent pricing from passengers
- **Multiple Income Streams** - Rides, courier delivery, and freight
- **Instant Payments** - Get paid via mobile money or cash

### Delivery Services
- **Courier Delivery** - Send packages up to 20kg across the city
- **Freight Services** - Book trucks for moving and cargo transport
- **Door-to-Door** - Direct delivery with live tracking
- **Proof of Delivery** - Confirmation on every package

## 🚀 How It Works

### For Passengers
1. **Set Location** - Enter pickup and destination
2. **Offer Your Price** - Set the fare you're willing to pay
3. **Choose Driver** - Compare offers and select your driver
4. **Track & Ride** - Live GPS tracking and safe arrival

### For Drivers
1. **Go Online** - Switch your status to online to receive requests
2. **Review Requests** - See ride and delivery requests nearby
3. **Accept or Negotiate** - Accept offers or make counter-offers
4. **Complete & Earn** - Complete trips and get paid

## 🎯 What Makes NegoRide Different?

### vs Traditional Ride-Hailing Apps
- **No Surge Pricing** - You set the price, not algorithms
- **Fair Negotiation** - Both passengers and drivers agree on the price
- **More Control** - Choose based on rating, vehicle, and price
- **Built for Liberia** - Local cities, local currency, local payment methods

### vs Existing Liberian Apps
- **Fare Negotiation** - Unique bidding system not found in Transix, LehGo, or Zuma
- **No Hidden Fees** - Transparent pricing from the start
- **Driver Flexibility** - Drivers can decline requests without penalty
- **Multi-Service** - Rides, delivery, and freight in one app
